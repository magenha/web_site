Why MAGENHA exists

A preservation project—not simply a search service.

Our work joins documentary evidence, uniparental genetics, and public education to keep family knowledge useful, testable, and open to future study.

01

Preserve genealogical knowledge

Recover, verify, and organize family history before fragile records, local knowledge, and living memory are lost.

02

Connect records with haplogroups

Place Y-DNA and mtDNA results in historical context through documented ancestors, dates, places, and chains of descent.

03

Protect lineages at risk

Prioritize direct paternal and maternal lines whose remaining testable representatives may otherwise disappear.

04

Invite the public into discovery

Promote interest in genealogy, genetics, archaeology, population history, and the fields that help explain human origins.

Genealogical research

From a single record to a defensible family line.

We research baptismal, marriage, and burial entries in Catholic ecclesiastical records; reconstruct family lines; and verify previous genealogies—especially where these records are difficult to access or unavailable online.

Lineage Research Program

Connect a haplogroup to a documented history.

If you or an appropriate relative already has—or independently obtains—a relevant Y-DNA or mtDNA result, MAGENHA can evaluate the corresponding direct paternal or maternal line.

When the lineage falls within the areas and periods we can research, the project may cover up to 100% of the standard genealogical work needed to document that specific uniparental line.

  • DNA testing is voluntary and never required for normal genealogy services.
  • You manage testing and the testing account entirely independently.
  • We do not request passwords, account access, raw DNA files, match lists, or private genetic information.
